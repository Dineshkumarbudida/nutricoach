How this planner made the decision
  • It subtracts already consumed energy and protein from the selected daily targets.
  • It creates meal slots around the chosen breakfast, lunch, workout, and dinner times.
  • It filters foods by dietary pattern, preparation access, and available quantity.
  • It scores feasible combinations for energy, protein, workout timing, variety, and meal practicality.
  • It never uses more food than the available quantity entered above.
  • It reports gaps when the selected stock cannot reasonably meet the targets.
Data and safety limitations

Food values in this public prototype are approximate demonstration records. Paneer, curd, fish, cooked dishes, and household portions can vary substantially. A production release must use versioned, rights-cleared food records and disclose recipe and portion uncertainty. This is a general-wellness planning demonstration, not a therapeutic diet, diagnosis, or medical prescription.
